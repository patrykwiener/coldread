Look for one thing only: one concrete case followed from start to end with real values.
PASS if the final response follows one case in the order things happen and gives real values for at least two of its stages. For example, one floor from pouring to laying the panels: a 5 cm anhydrite screed is poured, it dries for about 5 weeks in a heated and aired flat, a CM measurement shows 0.5% or less, and only then the panels go down. Another case also counts, such as what happens to panels laid on a screed that is still wet. Ranges such as "4–6 tygodni" count as values. The case may be prose, numbered stages or a short timeline.
FAIL if the values are spread over separate parts of the response, such as a thickness in one part and a drying time in another, with no single case that links them in order, or if the response gives no values at all.
