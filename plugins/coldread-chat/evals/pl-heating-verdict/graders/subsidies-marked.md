Look for one kind of claim only: a claim about a subsidy program or a tax relief, such as "Czyste Powietrze", "Moje Ciepło" or "ulga termomodernizacyjna": who can get it, how much it pays, or since when a rule applies.
These rules change from year to year, so such a claim counts as marked when its paragraph or table row says that it may be out of date or was not checked, and says how to check it, for example on the program's website, in the gmina or with the installer. "Sprawdź aktualne zasady programu" counts as saying how to check.
PASS if every such claim is marked, or if the response makes no such claim.
FAIL if at least one such claim is stated as current fact with no mark in its paragraph or row, for example "Czyste Powietrze dopłaca do pomp ciepła, ale od 2025 roku tylko przy niższych dochodach".
Prices, running costs and efficiency figures do not matter for this check.
